# Follow-up work

## P2 — Selective review

**What:** Reduce human review volume only after independent pilot validation,
research-lead approval, and drift monitoring.

**Why:** Full primary review is the launch safety boundary. Selective review can
save reviewer time later, but only model-, prompt-, form-, and schema-specific
calibration can justify it.

**Depends on:** Versioned calibration evidence, the ten-page Gemini comparison,
approved acceptance thresholds, and ongoing drift alerts.

## P2 — Calibrated crop geometry by census form

**What:** Validate row and field crop geometry for each year and schedule using
representative Ancestry scans.

**Why:** All cleaned Bastrop workbook schemas are supported now, but only 1950
has validated crop coordinates. Other forms safely use full-page extraction and
full-page review evidence until their geometry is measured.

**Depends on:** Authorized representative scans and cleaned matching physical
pages for each form.
